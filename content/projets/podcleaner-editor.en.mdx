- Studio sound: denoise, EQ, compression, mix at -16 LUFS.
- Shortens silences longer than one second.
- Removes isolated “uh”s and flags the rest.
- Finds film clips and spoken markers (“Claude, cut here”).
- Markdown edit file and markers for Logic Pro and Cakewalk.
