- Finds each line in the French subtitles, or in the dub with Whisper.
- Cuts snapped to sentences, with fades and a transition sound.
- Picks the French dub track (VFF/VFQ), normalises to -16 LUFS.
- 48 kHz WAV, H.264 MP4 and a `clips.json` index; only changed clips are redone.
- Optional Claude help to suggest cuts; works without it.
